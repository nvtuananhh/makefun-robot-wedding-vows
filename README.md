## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes
a one-line robot wedding vow to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I vow to love you through 214 firmware updates, every dead battery, and every reboot — and to never once call tech support on you.

The entry is 130 characters long. The vow is a real promise — to love
through every update, dead battery and reboot — and the laugh lands in
the final words: loyalty phrased as never calling tech support on your
own spouse, with a concrete number (214 updates) and the machine's own
world (firmware, battery, reboot, tech support). The triplet rhythm
(214 updates, dead battery, reboot) lets it read aloud in one breath,
deadpan, with the punch arriving last.

